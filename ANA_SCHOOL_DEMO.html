Personal Inspiration

Part of this project comes from real life, not only code. During seven years on Guernsey, I listened to Virgin Radio UK at work, on boats, and in everyday life. I keep it here as a small personal note about focus, memory, and the places where learning happens.

Privacy And Boundaries

This is a local educational demo. It should not collect personal data, publish private logs, expose keys, or inspect systems without permission. The goal is assistive tooling, diagnostics, and learning, not taking data from other people.
