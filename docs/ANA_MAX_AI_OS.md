# ANA MAX AI OS Concept

## Kernel

The runtime is the kernel: it schedules, routes, observes, and enforces policy.

## Processes

Agents are processes with roles, inboxes, lifecycle, and permissions.

## Syscalls

Tools are syscalls. They cross from reasoning into controlled action.

## Storage

Memory is storage: semantic, episodic, session-bound, and distributed.

## Permissions

Policies are permissions. Safe-mode, dev-mode, and write-mode define what a
process can do.
